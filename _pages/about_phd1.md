**Zafira Shabrina** <br>
She is a PhD candidate in Economic Psychology at Leiden University, supervised by Prof. Wilco van Dijk, Prof. Lotte van Dillen, and Dr. Mirre Stallen. Her research focuses on decision-making in financially vulnerable populations or those at risk of vulnerability through suboptimal decisions by exploring how people navigate uncertainty across two timescales: how psychological factors hinder self-employed workers from planning for retirement (long-term), and how income volatility shapes psychological states and saving decisions (short-term). Beyond her PhD, she is passionate about poverty research, particularly how hidden inequalities compound poverty. Besides her research work, she holds a position as a lecturer at Universitas Gadjah Mada, Indonesia.
