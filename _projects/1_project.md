Education opportunities are not equally divided, with students of lower 
socio-economic status being consistently disadvantaged. Halima's PhD research 
project is aimed at identifying how non-cognitive competences — such as academic 
self-efficacy — play a role in the education achievement gap (the relation between 
SES and performance), and how interventions designed to develop these competences 
can stimulate academic performance.
