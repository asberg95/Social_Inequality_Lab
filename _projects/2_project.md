**Anya Elisabeth Benson**

How does income volatility relate to well-being — including mental, physical, 
and social well-being? This scoping review project aims to extensively map out 
the relationships between income inequality and various well-being constructs. 

Through a comprehensive search of peer-reviewed journals, this project seeks to 
identify key findings, examine the methodologies employed, and highlight relevant 
research gaps in the field.
